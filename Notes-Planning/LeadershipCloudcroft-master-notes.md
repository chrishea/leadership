## Program Overview

Leadership Cloudcroft is a civic leadership program for the Village of Cloudcroft, NM. It builds engaged community leaders by exposing them to how the village operates, the people who run it, and the issues that shape its future.

## Year Two (2026) — Current Plan

*Source: planning meeting, Wed 22 Apr 2026. This is the most accurate, current view of the program.*

### Format Shift

- Year two moves from year one's class-based monthly format to a **"quasi think tank"** approach.
- Three main events instead of monthly sessions.
- Heavier emphasis on networking between participants and village decision makers.
- Less ambitious in scope than year one — designed in part to address year-one retention issues (about 50% dropout due to scheduling conflicts).

### Applications & Cohort

- Applications launch early May 2026, deadline end of May.
- Cohort announcement and the full event schedule go out at the same time, so participants can lock dates early.
- Open enrollment beyond village boundaries — including seasonal residents, business owners, and school families — to strengthen regional connections.

### The Three Sessions

**Session 1 — Meet & Greet (Mid-June)**

- Confirmed kickoff: **Thursday, June 11, 6–8 pm at The Distillery** (invite-only).
- Cocktail hour with introductions and brief programming.
- Food, two drink tickets, and a season preview provided.
- Includes a check-in with Southwest Sports on their plans for the ice rink.
- Audience: new cohort, alumni, village leaders, business figures.
- Featured guest speaker from the tourism sector (Shane Asbury or Corey Herman suggested).
- Backup/alternate venue considered: Ski Cloudcroft.

**Session 2 — Wells & Springs Tour (Tuesday, July 14)**

- **JJ** leading the water infrastructure tour (wells, springs, water tanks).
- Tour ends at **Ski Cloudcroft** for lunch and a presentation from ski-area owners on water/snow/Forest Service challenges.
- This is the signature field experience of the program.

**Session 3 — Responsible Tourism Forum (Mid-August)**

- Capstone event focused on sustainable tourism practices.
- Potential venues: Village chambers or an outdoor pavilion.
- Ground rules set up front to keep dialogue constructive.

### Communication & Logistics

- Group text for time-sensitive updates; email for detailed information.
- **Graduation:** informal coffee meeting at the library. $100 stipend and feedback collection. More casual than year one's formal event.

- 14 confirmed participants in the inaugural year.
- Roughly 50% dropout rate, attributed largely to scheduling conflicts — a key reason for the year-two redesign.

## Website Content Guidance

When generating site copy, communicate:

1. **What Leadership Cloudcroft is** — a civic leadership program building engaged village leaders, now in its second year.
2. **Year Two structure** — three signature events (Meet & Greet, Wells & Springs Tour with water-tanks field trip, Responsible Tourism Forum), positioned as a "quasi think tank" rather than a traditional class.
3. **The signature water-tanks field trip** — surface prominently; it's the most distinctive element of the year-two program.
4. **Who presents** — the program brings in the people actually running the village and the region (water operations, ski-area owners, tourism leaders, village officials), not outside lecturers.
5. **Who it's for** — village residents and broader regional stakeholders (seasonal residents, business owners, school families) committed to learning how Cloudcroft works.
6. **Application timing** — applications open early May 2026, close end of May; full schedule announced with the cohort.

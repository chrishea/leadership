## Article (verbatim)

If you've been curious about what makes Cloudcroft tick — its water supply, its seasonal economy, its long-term planning challenges — this is the program that takes you behind the scenes.

Leadership Cloudcroft (LC) brings together residents who want to understand their community more deeply and take action.

### What 2026 has in store

The 2026 cohort will visit Cloudcroft's wells and springs, connect with local leaders, and take part in a responsible tourism forum — organized by LC alumni and board members **Amy Coor** and **Rick Donnelly**, who are co-chairing this year's program.

### Meet your 2026 co-chairs and new LC Board Members

**Amy Coor** came to Cloudcroft the way many people do — on a camping trip, chasing snow. She loved it enough to keep coming back, and eventually to stay. In 2020, she and her family relocated from Artesia, and she's been deep in the fabric of this community ever since: Planning and Zoning Commission, Parks and Rec, Lodger's Tax Committee, volunteer firefighter, track coach, fitness instructor, and race director for the High Altitude Classic. She's also a 2025 LC alumna now stepping into a board role.

> "I'm grateful for the opportunity to give back to a community I truly love being a part of."
> — Amy Coor

**Rick Donnelly** grabbed two bags in 1983 and headed Southwest — and he's been exploring this part of the world ever since. He's been coming to Cloudcroft and the southern Sacramentos since 1998, found his way to Twin Forks in 2021, and settled in the village in 2024. A commercial phone tech for Verizon, he currently chairs the Planning and Zoning Commission and brings a grounded, practical perspective to everything he does. Also a 2025 LC alumnus, Rick is joining the board to help shape what 2026 becomes.

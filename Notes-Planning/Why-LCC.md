## 3. Rick Donnelly

**Areas of Interest:**
> Growth, fire safety, water use and savings.

**Why Participating:**
> I think the community has a strong position in geographic area for intelligent growth. To do so, thoughtful planning and zoning regs, water distribution and conservation, expand the size of village to bring in vested population for police/fire etc. Over this all, i have seen communities decimated by fire which still has yet to recover. From the fires in Yellowstone in 1988 to Paradise California and even the smaller town of Summerhaven AZ active prevention of fire is a must in a climate changing world.

## 12. Andrika Ruiz

**Areas of Interest:**
> I want to grow in my ability to serve our community effectively. I'm interested in learning ways to increase community involvement.

**Why Participating:**
> I'd like to participate in this year's Leadership Cloudcroft program because I'm deeply invested in this community. As a resident of High Rolls, what happens in Cloudcroft directly affects my family and me. I currently serve as Chair of the Cloudcroft Library Board, substitute teach at the middle and high school, am a local small business owner, and my spouse and I are church elders of Treehouse Fellowship here in Cloudcroft. I see firsthand how vital strong leadership is to the success of our community. I want to grow in my ability to serve effectively, avoid burnout, and help others do the same. This program is an opportunity to build the skills and relationships needed to strengthen not just Cloudcroft, but the mountain, for the future.

Year One · 2025

A foundation built on how the village really runs.

Last year's cohort didn't just sit in a room and listen to lectures — they got out. Fourteen participants spent six class days, June through October 2025, on the issues that most shape Cloudcroft's future: governance, infrastructure, fire, economic development, healthcare, and schools. Speakers included Village Attorney Zach Cook, municipal leaders Debbie Lee, Karen Gutierrez, and Dora, alongside local-government voices Gail and Tim, and grants specialist Lauren.

Field experiences ranged from a Wastewater Treatment Plant tour with Scott Powell and a deep-dive Emergency Management forum to a public mayoral debate between King and Wiley. The Cloudcroft Reader's launch announcement laid out the original brief: a formal pipeline to identify and prepare the volunteers who run a small mountain village. Year one shaped the year-two redesign — tighter, fewer scheduling conflicts, sharper focus.

About

A program built by Cloudcroft, for Cloudcroft.

Leadership Cloudcroft is a civic leadership program for the Village of Cloudcroft, New Mexico. We build engaged community leaders by exposing them to how the village operates, the people who run it, and the issues shaping its future — water, tourism, economic development, and the long-term health of a small mountain community. Beyond the cohort itself, the program is a pipeline into village service: internships in the water clerk, court, and administrative offices, and on-ramps to formal civic roles.
